# Cold Email Templates for Website Rebuild Outreach

## Strategy

**Goal**: Get them to click the demo link. Everything else is secondary.

**Why this works**: 
- Proof (live demo link) vs. mockup
- Urgency (you already built it)
- Specificity (show you analyzed their site)
- Clear CTA (one button: see the new site)

---

## What NOT to Do

❌ Don't say "let me know if you're interested"
❌ Don't include an attachment (email spam filters hate them)
❌ Don't use "check out my portfolio" (no credibility)
❌ Don't over-explain (keep it short)
❌ Don't ask for a call first (give them the link first)
❌ Don't use "I think you need a website" (insulting)
❌ Don't mention your competitors
❌ Don't use generic "web design" pitch

✅ DO focus on their problem (losing mobile traffic)
✅ DO show proof (live link, not mockup)
✅ DO be specific (mention their Google rating, niche)
✅ DO keep it short (2-4 sentences)
✅ DO give them an easy "yes" (one clear next step)

## Metrics to Track

Use AgentMail to track:

- **Open rate**: % who open the email (target: 30%+)
- **Click rate**: % who click the demo link (target: 20%+)
- **Reply rate**: % who reply or ask questions (target: 5-10%)
- **Meeting rate**: % who schedule a call (target: 10-15% of clickers)
- **Close rate**: % who become paying customers (target: 50%+)

### Revenue Math
```
100 emails sent
30 opens (30%)
6 clicks (20%)
1 meeting (15% of clickers)
0.5 closed deals (50% of meetings)
$5,000 average deal
= $2,500 expected revenue

Scale to 500 emails/month = $12,500/month revenue
```

## Pro Tips

1. **Send at 9-10 AM on weekdays** (highest open rate)
2. **Space emails 3 days apart** (consistent visibility)
3. **Personalize subject line** (mention their business name)
4. **Keep mobile responsive** (most emails opened on phone)
5. **Use short paragraphs** (easier to scan)
6. **Include your phone number** (builds credibility)
7. **Sign with your name** (feels more personal than "[Your Company]")
8. **Test subject lines** (A/B test on every batch)
